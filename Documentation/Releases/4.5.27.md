Irisin 4.5.27 is about a quarter smaller to download.

- The RootHide package was 6.6 MB, 1.9 MB of it six large copies of the app icon. The icon now ships as three compressed images, and the package is 4.8 MB. It looks the same on the Home Screen in Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted; it no longer moves with the light the way the layered glass icon did.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
