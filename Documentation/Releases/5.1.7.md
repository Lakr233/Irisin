Irisin 5.1.7 is a test build. It rebuilds an installed package into a `.deb` on your device. It is on this page only, and not in any repository yet.

- **Repack.** Reveal Files has a new menu at the top right. Repack builds a `.deb` from the files a package installed and hands it to the share sheet, with a progress bar and Cancel. The package keeps its control fields, scripts, file modes, owners and dates. Any changes made since installation go in as they are, and a notice says how many files changed or are missing.
- **Export List.** The same menu shares the file list as plain text.

A repacked package is not always the same file as the download. Its control fields are in dpkg's order, it is always compressed with xz, and it may have an `md5sums` the original did not ship. On RootHide, a package with a LaunchDaemon always shows one changed file, because RootHide rewrites the daemon's plist on the device. The repacked plist still loads.

Tested on RootHide in virtual machines, iPhone on iOS 26.4 and iOS 27.0.1. Every installed package repacked, 109 on iOS 26.4 and 98 on iOS 27.0.1. For 33 of them we compared the repack with the original from its repository, 10,367 entries in all: every file's bytes, mode, date and link target matched, apart from the rewritten plists and two `partial` folders apt changes itself. Repacked packages installed with `dpkg -i` and with `irisin-cli`, and `dpkg -V` found nothing changed afterwards. Cancel stopped a repack and left nothing behind. Rootless firmware was tested in the simulator only, with packages from Procursus. iPad and Apple TV were not tested on a device for this release.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware on iPhone and iPad, and `appletvos-arm64e` for RootHide on Apple TV. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
