Irisin 4.7.5 lines up lists in package descriptions on iOS 17 and earlier.

- **Lists line up on iOS 17 and earlier.** On iOS 15, 16 and 17, the first line of every list item in a package's description started a little to the right of the lines it wrapped onto. Every line of an item now starts in the same place. iOS 18 and later lay out as before.
- **Right click on blank space.** With a mouse or trackpad, a right click past the end of a line in a package's description no longer selects the last word on that line.
- **Updated libraries.** icli 0.9.2, which Irisin installs packages and registers apps with; libarchive 3.8.10 and xz 5.8.4, which read packages; MachOKit 0.55.0, which Compatibility Mode reads programs with.

Installed over 4.7.4 with irisin-cli and tested on iOS 26.4 and 27.0.1 with RootHide, in the iPhone and the iPad layout: the update of Irisin itself, a dry run, installing and removing a command-line package, installing and removing an app (on the iPad) and updating one (on the iPhone with iOS 27), Irisin registering its new build when it opens, the Dashboard, and a package description with a list. Lists on iOS 17 and earlier were not tested on a device for this release, and neither was rootless firmware.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
