Irisin 4.6.3 builds with icli 0.8.5.

- **icli 0.8.5.** The library Irisin registers apps and resprings with is declared from 0.8.5. That release changes how icli tells which app is in front on an iPad and lets icli's own test app launch on iOS 27; registering, unregistering and refreshing apps work as in 4.6.2. Tested on iOS 15.8.6 with a rootless bootstrap, and on iOS 26.6.2 and 27.0.1 with RootHide.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
