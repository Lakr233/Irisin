Irisin 4.5.25 opens again on RootHide setups where it quit as soon as it started.

- **Opens on every RootHide setup.** Since 4.5.23, Irisin keeps its data in a home folder inside the RootHide environment. Where that folder did not exist, Irisin quit right after launch. It now asks its background service to create the folder, then opens as usual.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
