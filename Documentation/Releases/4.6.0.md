Irisin 4.6.0 is built with icli 0.8.0, which refreshes the home screen faster and no longer stalls on some apps.

- **Installing or updating an app no longer stalls on its plug-ins.** An app whose `PlugIns` folder held something other than an app extension, such as a resource bundle, a framework or a plug-in without `NSExtension`, could never be confirmed as registered: the home screen keeps only app extensions there. Each registration then rechecked every installed app ten times, which took tens of seconds on a phone with many apps, and Rebuild Icons ended with an app it could not verify. Only app extensions are registered now, and only the app just registered is read back.
- **A refresh that cannot finish says what is missing.** It names the app and the part of its registration the home screen did not record, instead of counting failed and unverified apps.
- **icli 0.8.0.** The library Irisin installs packages with is declared from 0.8.0.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
