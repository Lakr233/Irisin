Irisin 5.0.3 makes the Apple TV screen quieter, asks the owner in tvOS's own alert, and lets Irisin on Apple TV open again after it updates itself from the browser. Nothing on screen changes on iPhone and iPad.

- **One quiet screen on Apple TV.** The QR code, then what to do, the address and the code, in one column and one size, told apart by weight alone. The title, the TV's name, the large blue digits and the daemon's path are gone; a line at the foot appears only while something installs or refreshes, or when Irisin can only browse.
- **The TV asks in its own alert.** A change a browser asks for comes up as tvOS's alert, with Decline focused and Menu declining, as before.
- **Irisin opens again after updating itself.** After an update from the browser page, tvOS refused to open Irisin on Apple TV: it had been registered while it was still running, which tvOS takes as disabling it. Irisin is now registered only once it has left. The helper the update installs does this, so it applies to the update from 5.0.2 as well.
- **Vision Pro.** The window fits what it shows instead of the system's wide default.
- **The browser page on a phone.** A repository's address gets a row of its own instead of a third of one.
- **The Apple TV package says what it is.** Its description no longer promises network management for a later release.

Tested on two Apple TV guests with RootHide, tvOS 26.6: an Apple TV 4K and an Apple TV HD. On both, the screen, and the owner's alert from the remote: Menu and Select on the focused Decline declining, Right and Select confirming, adding and deleting a repository. On the Apple TV 4K, updates of Irisin through the page, the registration after the app has left, and the app opening again; on the guests the launch after an update still needed that registration by hand, since the guest registers the app during the update itself. The Apple TV HD guest has no GPU and shows no QR code; a real one has a GPU. On iPhone (iOS 26.4 and 27.0.1) and iPad (iOS 26.4 and 27.0.1) with RootHide, installed over 5.0.0 and 5.0.1 with dpkg: Irisin opening, and a package installed and removed through `irisin-cli`. Rootless firmware and tvOS 17 were not tested.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware on iPhone and iPad, and `appletvos-arm64e` for RootHide on Apple TV. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
