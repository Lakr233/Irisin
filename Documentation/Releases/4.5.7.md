Irisin 4.5.7 moves to newer libarchive and icli packages so the app builds with Xcode 27. Browsing and installing work as before.

- Repository indexes and `.deb` files are read through libarchive.xcframework 1.0.0, whose Swift module is named ArchiveKit. The old name, LibArchive, differed from the C library's only by case, and on a case-insensitive disk Xcode 27 refused to load it. The C shim that worked around that is gone. The libarchive binary is the same one 4.5.6 shipped.
- The installer helper links icli 0.6.9 instead of 0.6.1, which brings icli's fixes since then, including the iOS 27 app-registration fallback and accurate reporting of an absent bootstrap.
- icli, libarchive and libsolv are declared as minimum versions, not exact ones.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
