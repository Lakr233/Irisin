Irisin 4.5.1 stays responsive while your repositories refresh.

- Pages that list packages no longer redraw every second while repositories download. They update when a repository finishes refreshing, since nothing they show changes before then.
- Lists of packages compare fewer details when they update, so the Dashboard, Installed and Updates pages redraw with less work.
- On iPad, the number next to Repositories in the sidebar shows how many are left while they refresh, such as 19/111.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
