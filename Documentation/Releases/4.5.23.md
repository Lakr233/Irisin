Irisin 4.5.23 registers apps with everything uicache registers for them, and is built with icli 0.7.17.

- **Apps keep their plug-ins and containers.** On iOS 27, an app installed into the bootstrap could be registered without its plug-ins, entitlements, app groups or data container. Irisin now registers each app with all of them and checks that the home screen kept the whole registration.
- **Rebuild Icons repairs incomplete registrations.** An app registered without part of its record, or updated to a new version with the same build number, is registered again. An app whose registration already matches is left alone, so a running app keeps running.
- **icli 0.7.17.** The library Irisin installs packages with now registers apps as uicache does.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
