Irisin 4.5.14 updates icli, which it uses to register apps and load its own daemon, to 0.7.4.

- **launchd replies are released.** Every launchd request icli made kept its reply, and the reply to a load holds the job's listening sockets. A daemon loaded through it could keep its port bound after it was unloaded, for as long as the process that loaded it was running.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
