Irisin 4.1.4 — a package page's photo arrives without a jump.

- A package page resizes its header photo in an animation. A photo already
  downloaded is there when the page opens; one that has to be fetched comes
  in after the page has settled.
- A request that leaves the queue as it is offers Open Queue in place of a
  Confirm that did nothing, and a package page whose package is queued says
  Open Queue and goes there.
- On iPhone the Repositories tab shows what onboarding added the first time
  it is opened after a fresh install.
- On iPad the operation sheet keeps its size when the log opens.
