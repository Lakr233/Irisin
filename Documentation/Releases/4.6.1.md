Irisin 4.6.1 runs on iOS 15.

- **iOS 15 and later.** The package now asks for iOS 15.0, down from 16.0. On iOS 16 and later Irisin works as it did. Tested on iOS 15.8.6 with a rootless bootstrap, and on iOS 26 and 27 with RootHide.
- **Procursus for iOS 15.** On iOS 15, the repositories Irisin recommends at first launch include Procursus's suite for that release (1800), on rootless and RootHide alike.
- **Translation where the system offers it.** On iOS 15, where the system translator is not available, Translate says so instead of translating.
- **icli 0.8.2.** The library Irisin installs packages with is declared from 0.8.2, the first release that runs on iOS 15.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
