Irisin 5.0.5 is made for iPhone Duo.

- **It fills the display.** Irisin is now built against the iOS 27.1 SDK. On iPhone Duo it used to sit in a frame on the screen; it now fills it, and its tabs and settings run down the side under the status bar.
- **The dashboard keeps clear of the side bar.** On the inner display the dashboard's rows were laid out for the whole width, so the last column ran under the bar at the side and the button beside Featured was covered. The rows now use the width the bar leaves, three across when there is room.

Tested on iPhone Duo (iPhone19,4, iOS 27.1) with RootHide in a virtual machine, closed and open: Irisin updated itself through `irisin-cli`, opened, and laid out its dashboard on both displays. Also tested on iPhone with RootHide, iOS 26.4 and 27.0.1: installed through `irisin-cli`, restarted its daemon, and opened. iPad, rootless firmware and Apple TV were not tested on a device for this release; the Apple TV package is built as before.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware on iPhone and iPad, and `appletvos-arm64e` for RootHide on Apple TV. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
