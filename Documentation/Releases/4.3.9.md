Irisin 4.3.9 is a translation release: six more languages, and nineteen in all.

- Turkish, asked for in issue 133, and Indonesian, Hindi, Bengali, Swahili and Yoruba with it. Every string the app says is translated in each of them — both catalogs and Irisin's page in the Settings app, 504 strings a language.
- Each language was settled before it was translated: a glossary of the app's own vocabulary first — Repository, Queue, Hold, Depiction, Respring, custom firmware — in the register Apple's own iOS uses for that language, then the strings against it, then one reading back over the whole language so a word decided once is not decided twice.
- Every translation keeps the format specifiers of the English, positional where the language puts the arguments in another order, and none of them says what the app never says.
- The README and the site's depiction said thirteen languages; they say nineteen.

Nothing else changed: no interface, no English wording, no behaviour.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
