Irisin 4.7.1 keeps Irisin open while it updates or removes itself.

- **Updating Irisin finishes on screen.** Telling the home screen about the new Irisin, which Irisin itself, its own setup script and the icon tools did in the middle of the update, can close the app before it shows how the update went. Irisin now leaves its own app alone until the update is over, and registers it when you tap Done and the app closes. If the app was closed before then, it finishes the job the next time you open it.
- **Removing Irisin shows the whole run.** Irisin's icon is taken off the home screen after the removal has ended, not halfway through it.
- **Package scripts can tell that Irisin is installing them.** Scripts run by Irisin find `IRISIN_INSTALLER_VERSION`, `IRISIN_INSTALLER_BUILD`, `IRISIN_INSTALLER_PROTOCOL` and `IRISIN_INSTALLER_CLIENT` (`app`, or `external` with `IRISIN_INSTALLER_TRANSACTION`) in their environment. Other package managers set none of them.
- **The installation log keeps every line.** Lines written by a package's setup script through Irisin could overwrite part of the log.

The update from 4.7.0 to 4.7.1 is still carried out by 4.7.0 and may close the app as before. Updates from 4.7.1 on are not affected.

Tested on iOS 26.6.2 and 27.0.1 with RootHide: updating from 4.7.0 in the app and installing over 4.7.1 with dpkg. Updating Irisin from 4.7.1 in the app and rootless firmware were not tested for this release.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
