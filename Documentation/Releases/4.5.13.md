Irisin 4.5.13 gathers a repository's actions into one menu on its page.

- **More on the repository page.** The share button is now a More menu that refreshes the repository, shares it (copy its address or APT source, share it, or export its packages or the repository itself), and deletes it after asking first.
- **The page closes when its repository is gone.** Deleting a repository from the list or the sidebar now closes its page if it is open, instead of leaving it on screen.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
