Irisin 4.5.21 fixes selecting text in package descriptions.

- **Selection handles drag again.** On iOS 18, the handles at the ends of a selection in a package's description could stay put while you dragged them, most often on iPad. They now follow your finger.
- **Text engine updated.** Package descriptions use Litext 3.5.0 and MarkdownView 4.7.0.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
