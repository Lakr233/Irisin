Irisin 4.3.0 opens straight to the interface, adds a queue bar and selection on the Installed page, and brings repository files to the Add Repository sheet.

- The app opens to its interface with no loading screen, and the tab bar no longer animates in after launch.
- A queue bar floats above the tab bar while packages are queued.
- The Installed page has swipe actions and multi-selection, which VoiceOver announces.
- Import a repository file from the Add Repository sheet, and export from Settings.
- Tap the Repositories tab again to refresh every repository.
- Share sheets on iPad always point at what was tapped.
- Package pages measure their rows without stalling, and repository rows show their images again.
- Reused rows no longer keep the previous row's look, including a removal's strikethrough in queue reports.
- Every string has its English text, and unused strings were removed.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
