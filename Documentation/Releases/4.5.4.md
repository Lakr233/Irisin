Irisin 4.5.4 keeps the queue responsive while your repositories refresh, and makes the iPad smoother.

- While repositories refresh, adding a package to the queue no longer waits for the refresh to finish. The queue checks your change again once, after the last repository is written.
- If a refresh updates or removes a package in your change while you review it, Confirm tells you which ones and offers to check the change again.
- Execute and Try Again no longer fail during a refresh when nothing in your queue has changed.
- On iPad, the sidebar no longer stutters as you scroll, and package pages open more smoothly.
- On a package page, a depiction tab that cannot be shown is left out and the page says some content cannot be shown, instead of opening an empty tab.
- A package's photo that finishes loading while you are on another page now appears at its full size when you come back.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
