Irisin 4.4.0 is smaller: the package falls from 9.6 MB to 5.7 MB, and installs in 21 MB instead of 37.

- The script reader keeps the bash grammar alone. It colours a shell script as before, and shows a script in any other language, such as Perl, Python, Ruby or Lua, as plain text. Those grammars came to 5 MB of the app's binary.
- Depictions no longer typeset math or colour code. A dollar sign in a depiction is text, and a code block keeps its frame and line numbers but draws plain. The fonts and stylesheets behind the two came to more than 7 MB.
- Runestone and MarkdownView are built from source inside the project and cut down to what Irisin uses. The License page lists their notices.
- The helper links icli 0.6.1. Installing, registering apps and respringing work as in 4.3.10.
- Every other dependency is at its newest release.

Nothing else changed: the interface, its wording and the way packages install are as they were.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
