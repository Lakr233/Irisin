- **Apple TV recommends OwnGoal's repository alone.** The list of repositories recommended on Apple TV (RootHide, `appletvos-arm64e`) is `https://apt.owngoal.dev`, the one repository that publishes Apple TV packages so far. It is read once Irisin has an app on Apple TV; the package still carries the daemon and the installer alone.

Built and checked by CI. Not tested on a device: nothing that runs on iPhone, iPad or Apple TV changed.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for RootHide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware on iPhone and iPad, and `appletvos-arm64e` for RootHide on Apple TV. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
